# 0017: Use the team's look, in the original layout

**Status:** accepted

## Decision

The app keeps its three-panel layout, restyled with UnderControl 1156's navy and blue, its logo, the Barlow font and icon tools.

## Why

The owner felt the first look was generic. A design canvas explored other layouts, including a full-window field, and the owner chose to keep this layout and change only its look.

## Consequences

The logo isn't covered by the project's MIT license. Fonts ship under the SIL Open Font License, with their licenses in `resources/third-party`. Anyone reusing the app for another team would swap the logo and colors.
